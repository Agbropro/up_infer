METRIC GUIDE

Confidence & IoU

Confidence

The model’s certainty that a detected object belongs to a class. A higher setting hides uncertain predictions; a lower setting shows more possibilities.

Intersection over Union (IoU)

How much two predicted areas may overlap before they are treated as duplicates. A lower setting removes overlapping predictions more aggressively.
